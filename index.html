Part 2 · The 3PL question

"Why can't Increff fulfil the orders?" Because we're not a 3PL in Thailand.

What a 3PL sells

Warehouse space + manpower + transport + its own WMS, as one service.

What Increff sells

The software layer: WMS, OMS, SFS and merchandising, licensed to the company that runs the operation.

Bata Thailand was choosing between three 3PLs offering warehouse + manpower + WMS + OMS. Increff can't bid on that.

So when the brand outsources to a 3PL, ask:

If you hear…It meansDo this
"Our 3PL runs the warehouse, their WMS is fine"The brand isn't the WMS buyerDon't book WMS
"We can't see 3PL stock with store & online stock"Multi-channel inventory painOMS meeting
"Allocation to stores is in Excel"Planning pain (GO Wholesale had this)MS meeting
"We'll take the warehouse back in-house"Future WMS, with a trigger dateNurture to the date
GO Wholesale: 14 stores, own warehouse only at 20–25 stores (~2 yrs). Bata: decision end-Nov 2026, through Bata Global.

Exceptions exist where urgency or complexity is high. Flag them to Increff before booking.

Book with

Head level and above: Founder / CXO · Heads of Supply Chain, Operations, Warehouse, Logistics, E-commerce, Merchandising, Planning, Buying · IT / Digital leaders

Not the primary contact

Managers, junior coordinators and analysts · stand-ins for the real owner · engineers only · procurement with no business owner · consultants without the client on the call

Thai examples: Ari Football's first call was an ERP stand-in; M-SENKO sent only engineers; Pranda's decision-maker dropped off.

A workshop is not a POC. Most of what we've called "POC" in Thailand was a workshop.

PaperworkNone (NDA only if their data is shown)Signed LOI (intent to buy if criteria are met) + data NDA + POC charterLOI + NDA, and Business Head / CRO approval

Success criteriaProspect confirms fit3–5 measurable criteria in writing (e.g. forecast accuracy, sell-through vs control stores)Same
DurationA day or two≤ 90 days incl. data onboarding; clock starts when data arrivesSame

HomePro asked to "test the system" with sample data before sharing theirs → that's a workshop / sandbox, not a POC.

ICC: AO proposed "stage 1 POC on dummy data". Increff's view: that's a demo already done. POC starts only with ICC's data, NDA and the right stakeholders.

Part 6 · Playbook

How we source in Thailand from here, and in any new region

Target

  • Multi-brand / multi-store fashion & lifestyle retailers (100+ stores) planning in Excel → MS
  • Beverage, food and FMCG distributors running their own warehouses → WMS 2.0
  • Fashion brands with own warehouse, B2C + marketplaces, ≥30k pieces/month → WMS 1.0 + OMS
  • Companies with a trigger: new warehouse, contract ending, current system too costly or not scaling

Avoid

  • Warehouse fully outsourced to a 3PL with no OMS or planning pain
  • ERP projects in flight, or "our ERP can't do X"
  • Systems that went live in the last 12 months
  • Single-store or very small assortments
  • Manufacturing / production-planning asks

How we run it

  • 2-minute website + LinkedIn pre-check before outreach
  • Route the product before booking; name the meeting after the real pain
  • Send the handoff form with the invite; no form, no booking
  • Confirm the decision-maker 24 hours before; reschedule rather than run without them
  • AO joins as an extended team member who probes, not only a translator
  • Probe for BANT: use the call to get the answers missing at booking (budget, decision-maker, need, timeline). Don't pitch features; leave the product to the Increff team
